Unit Types

Every Movable Unit, Built to Purpose

Each unit is designed around the specific operational requirement it serves — never a generic off-the-shelf piece.

Service Stations

Your floor team's operational hub. Service stations on lockable castors carry linen, cutlery, condiments, POS equipment, and order dockets. We design them for your specific service style — whether you're running fine dining, casual dining, or café service.

Hostess Desks

First impressions matter. Our hostess and reception desks are designed to command the entrance — with integrated reservation book storage, tablet arm brackets, and a finish that matches your brand identity precisely. Available in fixed and semi-movable configurations.

Vanity Counters

Restaurant restrooms are a direct reflection of your brand's attention to detail. Our vanity counters feature precision basin cutouts, under-basin storage, integrated mirror frames, and surface finishes ranging from stone-effect laminates to genuine marble composites.

Trash Bin Enclosures

Exposed waste bins are an eyesore in any well-designed dining space. Our bin enclosures conceal standard commercial bins in timber or laminate housings with easy-access flip-top lids, base castors for easy cleaning, and finishes that blend with your interior.

Bar Trolleys

Elegant mobile beverage service trolleys — for wine service in fine dining, cocktail carts in bars, and dessert trolleys in special dining formats. Finished to specification with brass, black metal, or natural timber detailing.

Condiment & Cutlery Stations

Hygienic, well-organised condiment dispensing stations for QSR and casual dining — keeping sauces, cutlery, napkins, and straws accessible to guests without visual clutter. Designed for easy refilling and daily cleaning.
