Façade & Exterior Solutions

The façade is where brand meets street. FlatPackCo designs and builds complete exterior treatments — from aluminium composite and timber cladding systems to full-height glazing, retractable canopies, and illuminated entrance features. Every façade is engineered to withstand local weather conditions while delivering the visual impact that draws foot traffic through the door. We handle structural calculations, municipal approvals, and installation as part of the package. Read our Restaurant Façade Guide for help planning your storefront.

Façade Systems We Deliver

  • ACP Cladding — aluminium composite panels in any RAL colour, with clean joint detailing and concealed fixings
  • Timber & WPC Cladding — natural wood or wood-polymer composite for warm, textured exteriors
  • Full-Height Glazing — toughened glass shopfronts with steel or aluminium framing for maximum visibility
  • Canopies & Awnings — retractable fabric, fixed metal, and glass canopy systems for outdoor seating areas
  • Entrance Portals — branded entrance features with integrated signage, lighting, and material layering
  • Louvre & Screen Systems — decorative and functional screening for ventilation, privacy, and sun control

Design Approach

From Street View to Statement

We treat every façade as an architectural brand statement — not just a weatherproof skin.

Street Presence

Every façade is designed to catch attention from maximum distance — material choices, colour contrasts, and lighting positions are all calculated for street-level impact.

Weather Engineering

Monsoon drainage, UV-resistant finishes, and wind-load calculations are built into every design — your façade looks as good in year three as it did on opening day.

Compliance & Approvals

We handle municipal signage permits, structural certifications, and fire-safety compliance as part of the façade package — no surprises after installation.
