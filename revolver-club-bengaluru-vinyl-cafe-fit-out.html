Project Overview

A Vinyl Café That Gets Quieter Toward the Back

The Revolver Club sells vinyl records, turntables, hi-fi and home-audio equipment, and runs listening sessions for its community of music lovers. Its Bengaluru store in Indiranagar brings all of that under one roof: a bright café and browsing floor at the front, a raised performance stage and turntable stations in the middle, and a dedicated hi-fi listening room at the back.

The space was designed by our sister brand SprintCo and built by FlatPackCo. Our scope covered the fixed joinery, acoustic treatment, metalwork, glazing and doors, delivered through a main package and two additional packages between June and October 2026.

Café and entrance. An 80mm curved café counter partition on an MS/aluminium frame, clad in fire-retardant HDHMR board and flexible ply, with a POS counter and work table, an equipment back counter, overhead storage and a perforated metal mesh fascia above. The entrance pairs acoustic double-glazed (DGU) fixed glazing and a DGU double door with a collapsible rolling shutter.

Stage and record retail. Suspended timber ceiling bands with integrated lighting and solid wood flooring frame the performance stage, with a bulkhead for the projector screen. The retail floor carries record displays and storage, rubber-wood record bins, comic and book shelving backed with metal mesh, a powder-coated showcase for accessories, wainscot panelling and a peg wall.

Hi-fi room. The quietest room in the store has 25mm stretched acoustic fabric wall panelling, an acoustic coffered timber ceiling, solid wood flooring under the AV system, soundproof fixed glazing, a solid-core acoustic door and motorised blackout blinds.

How We Did It

One Store, Three Very Different Sound Zones

1
Café & Retail Joinery

Curved counters, storage, record displays and bins, book shelving and metal showcases, built in fire-retardant HDHMR board and powder-coated MS for daily retail use.

2
Stage & Ceilings

Suspended timber ceiling bands with integrated lighting, solid wood stage flooring and bulkheads for the projector screen and performance area.

3
Acoustic Hi-Fi Room

Stretched fabric acoustic panelling, a coffered timber ceiling, soundproof glazing and an acoustic door, so the listening room stays separate from the café.
