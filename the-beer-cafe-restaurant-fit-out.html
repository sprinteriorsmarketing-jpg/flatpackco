Project Overview

From Empty Shell to Opening Night in 18 Days

The Beer Cafe approached FlatPackCo with a familiar but exacting challenge: a brand-new Mumbai outlet needed to be ready to trade in under three weeks — without compromising the signature industrial-meets-warm aesthetic that defines their customer experience.

Every element, from the back-bar millwork and communal high tables to the suspended baffle ceilings and illuminated façade signage, was fabricated simultaneously in our controlled workshop environment. On-site, our assembly team worked in parallel with services contractors, compressing what would have been a 12-week traditional build into an 18-day sprint.

The result is a space that feels both unmistakably Beer Cafe and uniquely Mumbai — and that opened on the date the client's marketing campaign had already promised.

How We Did It

Three Parallel Workstreams, One Seamless Handover

1
Days 1–5 — Design Lock & Workshop Fabrication Begins

All millwork, baffle elements, furniture, and signage fabricated simultaneously to locked drawings.

2
Days 4–12 — Site Prep Runs in Parallel

Floor screeding, electrical rough-in, plumbing services, and surface finishes completed on-site while fabrication continues off-site.

3
Days 13–18 — Assembly, Snagging & Handover

Workshop elements delivered and assembled with precision. Punch-list cleared on Day 17. Keys handed over on Day 18.
