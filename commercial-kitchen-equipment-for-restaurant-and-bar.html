Commercial Kitchen Equipment

The kitchen is the engine of every food business. FlatPackCo sources, specifies, and installs commercial kitchen equipment for restaurants, hotels, cloud kitchens, QSR chains, and institutional cafeterias. From heavy-duty cooking ranges and walk-in cold rooms to exhaust hoods, prep tables, and warewashing systems — we work with leading manufacturers to deliver the right equipment at competitive pricing, installed correctly and ready to run from day one. Our in-house stainless steel workshop also fabricates custom tables, shelving, and exhaust ducting to your exact kitchen layout.

Equipment Categories We Supply

  • Cooking Equipment — burner ranges, tandoors, griddles, fryers, combi ovens, and charcoal grills
  • Refrigeration — reach-in fridges, freezers, under-counter units, blast chillers, and walk-in cold rooms
  • Prep Equipment — food processors, mixers, slicers, vegetable cutters, and dough sheeters
  • Exhaust & Ventilation — SS hoods, grease filters, exhaust ducting, fresh air systems, and fire suppression
  • Warewashing — undercounter, hood-type, and conveyor dishwashers plus pot wash stations
  • SS Fabrication — custom work tables, shelving units, sinks, trolleys, and pass-through counters
  • Beverage Equipment — espresso machines, ice machines, juice dispensers, and beer tap systems

Our Approach

From Menu to Machine

We don't sell equipment from a catalogue. We specify what your kitchen actually needs based on your menu, volume, and space.

Menu-Based Specification

Your equipment list starts with your menu — we calculate production requirements, peak output, and workflow before recommending a single piece of equipment.

Competitive Sourcing

We work with multiple manufacturers and importers to get the best price-to-performance ratio — no tied suppliers, no inflated margins, just the right equipment at the right price.

Install & Commission

Every piece of equipment is installed, connected, tested, and commissioned by our team — gas, electric, plumbing, and extraction all coordinated to fire up on schedule.
