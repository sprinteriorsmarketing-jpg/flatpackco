Signage Systems

Signage is how your brand speaks when you're not in the room. FlatPackCo designs, fabricates, and installs the full spectrum of commercial signage — from illuminated external brand signs and neon feature pieces to internal wayfinding, menu boards, and digital display integrations. Every sign is built for longevity, visibility, and brand consistency, with materials and finishes that hold up in Indian climate conditions. We handle design, fabrication, electrical, municipal permits, and installation end to end. Read our Restaurant Signage Guide for help planning signage across your space.

Signage Types We Deliver

  • Illuminated Brand Signs — front-lit, back-lit, and halo-lit logo signs in acrylic, stainless steel, or ACP
  • Neon & Neon Flex Signs — custom neon tube and LED neon flex signs for interiors, bars, and feature walls
  • Totem Poles & Pylons — freestanding branded structures for parking lots, highway visibility, and multi-tenant buildings
  • Wayfinding Systems — directional signs, room identifiers, and floor-level navigation for large venues
  • Digital Menu Boards — screen-mounted or integrated digital displays with remote content management
  • Window & Wall Graphics — frosted film, vinyl wraps, and printed graphics for glass and interior surfaces
  • ACP & Metal Fascia Signs — clean, durable fascia-mounted brand signage for storefronts and malls

Design Approach

Signage That Works Day and Night

We design every sign to perform in both daylight and after dark — visibility, readability, and brand impact around the clock.

Daytime Visibility

Material selection, colour contrast, and sizing are calculated for maximum legibility at street-level distances — your sign works just as hard in full sunlight as it does when lit.

Night Illumination

LED modules, neon flex, and backlit panel systems are engineered for even light distribution, energy efficiency, and long service life — no dark spots, no hot spots.

Built to Last

UV-resistant acrylics, marine-grade stainless steel, and weatherproof electrical components ensure your signage survives monsoons, dust, and daily sun exposure year after year.
