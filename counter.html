Counter Types We Fabricate

  • Bar Counters — curved, straight, L-shaped, and island configurations

Design Approach

Every Counter, Engineered for Its Purpose

We don't build off a catalogue. Every counter is designed around your operation, your brand, and your floor plan.

Bar Counter Design

A great bar counter is a theatre piece. We engineer bar tops for visual impact — curved facades, underlit overhangs, feature stone tops, and smart under-bar layouts that keep your team fast and organised.

Café Counter Design

Your ordering counter sets the tone the moment a customer walks in. We integrate display cases, pastry shelves, POS positions, and branding elements into a single cohesive unit built for café service flow.

QSR Counter Engineering

Speed and hygiene are non-negotiable in QSR. We build counters with easy-clean surfaces, tray slide rails, equipment integration, and crew-side organisation that keeps your service line efficient at peak hours.

Cold Display Integration

Refrigerated display counters and cold wells built directly into your counter unit — perfect for gelato bars, cake counters, and deli-style service formats that merchandise product while keeping it fresh.

POS & Tech Integration

We design every counter around your technology — cable management conduits, screen mounting points, tablet arm brackets, card reader recesses, and power points positioned for clean, clutter-free service.

Delivery Counter Zones

Dedicated delivery collection counters that separate delivery riders from dine-in guests, with order display screens, bag staging shelves, and branding that keeps the delivery experience on-brand.
