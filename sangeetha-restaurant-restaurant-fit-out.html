Sangeetha Veg Restaurant
Mylapore, Chennai

A multi-scope interior supply for one of South India's most iconic vegetarian restaurant brands — 119 dining chairs, 7 sofa types, 3 private dining rooms fitted out, Rangoli pattern wall units, decorative ceiling rafters, 220m neon strip lighting, and a retail display suite at Mylapore, Chennai.

LocationMylapore, Chennai
CategoryVegetarian Restaurant
ZonesDining, Retail, 3× PDR
Year2024
Project Overview

A Heritage Brand Gets a Contemporary Interior to Match Its Legacy

Sangeetha Veg Restaurant on South Mada Street, Mylapore is one of Chennai's most beloved dining institutions — serving generations of families with authentic South Indian vegetarian cuisine. This fit-out was an opportunity to honour that heritage while creating a space worthy of the brand's next chapter.

FlatPackCo delivered a comprehensive multi-scope supply covering four distinct workstreams: loose furniture (119 chairs in 7 types, 21 sofas across 6 styles, 10 table configurations), interior architectural elements (33 items across ceiling rafters, Rangoli pattern wall units, decorative columns, planter boxes, and PDR room fit-outs), a full neon and wall washer lighting package, and a retail display suite for the restaurant's front-of-house retail zone.

The three private dining rooms (PDR 1, 2, and 3) each received bespoke ceiling elements, wall panelling, and a dedicated service station — ensuring that every private event at Sangeetha is held in a space that reflects the same care and craft as the main dining hall. The Rangoli pattern wall units (5 nos across 2 types) introduce a culturally rooted visual identity element that connects the interior design to its Mylapore context.

How We Did It

Three Parallel Scopes, One Coordinated Delivery

1
Interior Elements & Woodwork

All 33 interior elements — Rangoli walls, ceiling rafters, PDR panelling, retail display counters, wainscot — designed, drawn, and fabricated against shop drawings approved within one week of PO.

2
Furniture Production

119 chairs and 21 sofa units across 7 and 6 types respectively — all fabric selections from Solanki Morocco and French collections confirmed before cutting. Long booth sofas produced to dimension with Valencia Ash laminate backing.

3
Lighting Package

Flexi Neon strips, wall washers, and Meanwell drivers dispatched after site readiness confirmation. 220m total strip run coordinated with electrical layout to ensure uniform 3000K warm-white output across all dining zones.
