System Families

Eight Ceiling System Families

Each system creates a distinct visual language — choose based on your space, acoustics, and brand direction.

Linear Slat Ceilings

Timber or aluminium batten systems suspended from a carrier grid. One of the most popular ceiling choices for cafés, casual dining, and retail — warm, natural, and versatile across stain colours and spacing patterns.

Exposed Rafter & Framework Ceilings

Raw timber beams, engineered wood, or mild steel painted to specification. The honest, confident aesthetic that defines brewpubs, sports bars, concept restaurants, and industrial-style retail environments.

Coffered & Grid Ceilings

Sunken panel grid patterns — classically associated with premium hospitality and fine dining. Modern takes in dark timber or matte black metal make this system equally compelling in contemporary spaces.

Decorative Panel Ceilings

Shaped, textured, and cladded surface panels that introduce visual depth and brand character overhead. Available in gypsum, timber veneer, metal, and composite materials with custom finish options.

Acoustic Ceilings

Perforated metal, mineral fibre, and padded fabric ceiling systems engineered to absorb sound and reduce reverberation. Essential for high-occupancy restaurants, QSR environments, and open-plan retail spaces.

Floating Island & Feature Ceilings

Suspended ceiling elements — timber rafts, metal frames, and fabric canopies — that drop into a space to anchor zones, define intimacy, and layer the ceiling plane without covering the full area.

Backlit & Illuminated Ceilings

LED-integrated panels, frosted acrylic rafts, and tensioned fabric with lighting arrays that make the ceiling itself a light source. Creates ethereal, premium ambience for cocktail bars, fine dining, and flagship retail.

Parametric & Sculptural Ceilings

CNC-cut, 3D-formed, and algorithmically shaped ceiling elements that create signature overhead moments. Used for hero entrances, brand flagship environments, and concept spaces where no ceiling looks like another.
