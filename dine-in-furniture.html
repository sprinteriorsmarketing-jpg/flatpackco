Furniture Types

Every Seating Solution, Designed for Its Role

From the individual chair to a full banquette system — every piece is engineered for commercial performance.

Dining Chairs

The workhorse of any dining room. We fabricate dining chairs in timber, bentwood, metal, and fully upholstered constructions — all with commercial-grade frames designed to withstand thousands of cycles of daily use without compromising on appearance or comfort.

Booth & Banquette Seating

Fixed booth seating creates the intimate, cocoon-like dining experience guests remember. Our booths are fully upholstered in commercial-grade fabric or leatherette, built with solid timber frames and high-density foam, and available in any configuration from two-seat to extended wall-run banquettes.

Restaurant Sofas

For casual dining, café lounge areas, and social dining concepts, our restaurant sofas combine the comfort of residential design with the durability required in commercial environments — removable seat pads, stain-resistant upholstery, and reinforced frames as standard.

Bar Stools

Counter-height and bar-height stools in fixed and swivel configurations — for bar tops, high tables, and counter seating arrangements. Available with or without backs, in timber, metal, and upholstered options, with footrest rails positioned for comfort at your specific counter height.

Dining Tables

Table tops fabricated in laminate, compact HPL, stone-effect composites, or solid timber — on steel, cast iron, or timber bases. We supply tables to fit your exact layout: square, round, rectangular, and extendable options in any size your floor plan requires.

Outdoor Seating

Weather-resistant dining furniture for terraces, rooftops, and al fresco areas — powder-coated aluminium frames, teak and treated timber tops, and UV-stable upholstered cushions that maintain their appearance in the toughest outdoor conditions.

Finish & Upholstery

Customised to Your Brand Palette

Every fabric, finish, and frame colour is chosen in consultation — ensuring your furniture is a natural extension of your brand identity.

Custom Fabric Upholstery

Choose from a curated selection of commercial-grade fabrics — woven, velvet, boucle, and outdoor-safe textiles in any colour from your brand palette.

Vinyl & Leatherette

Highly durable, easy-clean vinyl and PU leatherette upholstery — the practical choice for high-spill environments without compromising on aesthetic quality.

Timber Finishes

Natural, stained, painted, and lacquered timber options — from light Scandinavian oak tones to deep espresso walnut finishes that suit any interior direction.

Metal Frames

Powder-coated steel and aluminium frames in matte black, brushed gold, chrome, and any RAL colour — for an industrial, contemporary, or classic look.
