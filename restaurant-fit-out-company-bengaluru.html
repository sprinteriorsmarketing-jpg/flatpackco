Bengaluru Fit-Outs

Factory-Built Restaurant Interiors, Installed in Bengaluru

FlatPackCo delivers restaurant fit-outs in Bengaluru by making key components at our factory in Wada and assembling them on site. We have completed projects in Indiranagar, Electronic City and Wood Street.

This is how we deliver outside our Mumbai base: drawings are finalised early, components are manufactured while site work progresses, and the finished pieces are transported to your unit for assembly. At Harvest Salad & Co in Indiranagar, that took a stripped concrete shell to an opening-ready café in 15 days.

Fifteen days is not a fixed timeline for every project. Scope, site readiness, approvals and design decisions all affect the programme, and we set out a realistic plan once we have seen yours.

How We Deliver

How a Bengaluru Project Runs

01

Brief and Site Details

You share your concept, layout, location and target opening date, so we can assess scope and timing.

02

Design and Shop Drawings

We develop the design and detailed drawings, fixing dimensions and materials before anything is made.

03

Manufacturing at Wada

Counters, panels, ceilings and furniture are made at our factory while site work moves ahead in parallel.

04

Transport and Assembly

Finished components are transported to Bengaluru and assembled on site, reducing on-site fabrication.

05

Inspection and Handover

We check the finished space against the approved design before handing it over for opening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver restaurant fit-outs in Bengaluru?

Yes. Our Bengaluru projects include Harvest Salad & Co in Indiranagar, Social at Electronic City and The Hood by Olive on Wood Street.

Do you have an office in Bengaluru?

Our office is in Andheri East, Mumbai, and our factory is in Wada. We deliver Bengaluru projects by manufacturing components at the factory, transporting them and assembling them on site.

Can you fit out a restaurant in Bengaluru in 15 days?

Some projects can, depending on scope, site condition, design and approvals. Harvest Salad & Co in Indiranagar went from bare shell to opening-ready in 15 days, but this is not a fixed timeline for every project.

What types of restaurants do you fit out in Bengaluru?

We have delivered a QSR and café, a large bar and café, and a multi-zone bar and kitchen in the city, and we also take on restaurants, kiosks and retail food formats.

How do I get a quote for a Bengaluru project?
