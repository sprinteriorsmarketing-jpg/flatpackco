What Is FlatPackCo? A Complete Guide to Its Design & Build Process

Building a restaurant, café, bar, or retail space can be complex. FlatPackCo offers a different approach — one integrated process from design to installation.

Building a restaurant, café, bar, or retail space can be complex. It often involves coordinating multiple vendors, managing timelines, handling site challenges, and ensuring the final space matches the original design. This is where FlatPackCo offers a different approach.

FlatPackCo is a design-and-build company that specialises in modular construction for hospitality and commercial spaces. By integrating design, engineering, manufacturing, and on-site execution, FlatPackCo simplifies the project journey and helps businesses build faster, more efficiently, and more consistently.

What Is FlatPackCo?

FlatPackCo is a modular Design & Build company focused on creating restaurants, cafés, bars, kiosks, retail stores, and commercial interiors. Instead of relying entirely on traditional on-site construction, FlatPackCo manufactures key interior components in a controlled factory environment before assembling them on-site.

This approach improves quality, reduces construction time, and delivers consistent results across projects, making it particularly valuable for businesses planning multiple outlets or franchise expansions.

How Does the FlatPackCo Process Work?

FlatPackCo follows an integrated process that keeps every stage of the project connected. (New to the model? Start with What Is Design & Build?)

01

Design Planning

Every project begins with understanding the client's vision, business goals, operational requirements, and customer experience. Detailed layouts and space planning ensure the design is both functional and visually appealing.

02

Engineering & Shop Drawings

Once the design is approved, detailed shop drawings are prepared. These drawings define every component, dimension, material, and connection, ensuring precision during manufacturing and installation.

03

Factory Manufacturing

Furniture, counters, wall panels, ceilings, storage units, and other interior elements are manufactured in FlatPackCo's production facility using precision-engineered processes. Factory manufacturing helps maintain consistent quality while reducing on-site work.

04

Site Preparation

While manufacturing is underway, the site team completes civil work, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and other essential services. Running these activities in parallel helps shorten the overall project timeline.

05

Installation & Finishing

The prefabricated components are transported to the site and assembled with precision. This results in faster installation, cleaner execution, and a high-quality finish.

Why Choose a Modular Design & Build Process?

A modular approach offers several practical advantages:

  • Faster project completion
  • Better quality control
  • Reduced on-site disruption
  • Improved budget predictability
  • Consistent execution across multiple outlets
  • Easier scalability for growing brands

This makes modular construction an ideal solution for hospitality businesses looking to expand efficiently. For multi-outlet brands, our Restaurant Rollout Guide explains how this scales.

Final Thoughts

FlatPackCo combines design, engineering, manufacturing, and construction into one streamlined process. By shifting much of the work to a controlled factory environment, projects can be delivered with greater speed, consistency, and quality than many traditional construction methods.

For businesses planning a new commercial space or expanding across multiple locations, an integrated design-and-build approach can simplify execution while creating spaces that are built to perform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FlatPackCo?

FlatPackCo is a modular Design & Build company specialising in restaurants, cafés, bars, kiosks, retail stores, and commercial interiors. It combines design, engineering, manufacturing, and on-site execution into a single integrated process.

How does FlatPackCo's process work?

The process includes five key stages: design planning, engineering, factory manufacturing, site preparation, and on-site installation. This integrated workflow helps improve efficiency and quality.

What makes FlatPackCo different from traditional contractors?

Unlike traditional construction, FlatPackCo manufactures many interior components in a factory before installing them on-site. This reduces construction time, improves quality control, and ensures greater consistency.

What types of projects does FlatPackCo handle?

FlatPackCo designs and builds restaurants, cafés, bars, QSRs, cloud kitchens, airport kiosks, retail stores, food courts, and other commercial spaces.

What are the benefits of modular construction?

Modular construction offers faster project delivery, better quality control, less on-site disruption, predictable timelines, reduced rework, and easier expansion for multi-location businesses.

Is FlatPackCo suitable for restaurant chains and franchises?

Yes. FlatPackCo's modular approach is particularly well suited for franchise brands and restaurant chains because it enables consistent design, standardised manufacturing, and faster rollout of multiple outlets.

Does FlatPackCo provide both design and construction?

Yes. FlatPackCo follows an integrated design-and-build model, managing engineering, manufacturing, procurement, and project execution under one coordinated process.
