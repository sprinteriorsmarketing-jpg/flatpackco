Explore the Latest Restaurant Interior Design Trends

Restaurant design today goes far beyond aesthetics — it's about creating memorable experiences by blending style, comfort, sustainability, and technology to help your space compete and stand out.

Restaurant design has always been about more than how a space looks. Today, it's about creating memorable experiences — spaces that integrate style, comfort, sustainability, and technology to help establishments compete effectively in a crowded market. Here are the design movements currently shaping the most talked-about restaurant interiors.

01

Biophilic Design

Living walls, potted plants, and natural materials like wood and stone are being used to bring the outdoors in. Beyond the visual appeal, biophilic design establishes a calming atmosphere for diners while genuinely improving air quality — a combination that's hard to beat for hospitality spaces.

02

Flexible Seating Arrangements

Modular furniture that can be rapidly reconfigured for different group sizes is becoming standard rather than a nice-to-have. It optimises space efficiency and customer flow, letting a single floor plan adapt smoothly from a quiet weekday lunch to a busy weekend with large groups.

03

Bold Colors and Patterns

Restaurants are moving beyond safe, neutral palettes in favour of vibrant hues and eye-catching designs — expressed through tiles, wallpaper and wall panelling, and upholstery. Used thoughtfully, bold colour and pattern create spaces that feel energetic and distinctly memorable.

04

Open Kitchens

Open kitchens continue to grow in popularity by giving diners transparency into food preparation and connecting them directly with the culinary process. The theatre of an open kitchen adds an experiential layer that a closed-off back-of-house simply can't replicate.

05

Sustainable Materials

Recycled, reclaimed, and low-impact materials — including bamboo flooring and organic fabrics — are increasingly prioritised in restaurant fit-outs. As guests become more environmentally conscious, the materials a space is built from are becoming part of its story.

Why Design Trends Matter for Your Business

These aren't just aesthetic choices — design influences customer behaviour and operational outcomes in measurable ways:

  • Extended visit duration and higher spend per guest
  • Stronger brand differentiation in a crowded market
  • Improved staff efficiency through smarter layouts
  • Greater adaptability to changing market demands
  • Increased appeal to environmentally conscious diners

The 60/40 Design Rule

One simple principle that helps tie a space together is the 60/40 rule: allocate around 60% visual dominance to one primary colour or material, and use the remaining 40% for accents. This creates harmony throughout the space without tipping into monotony.

Technology Integration

Technology is increasingly woven into the design itself rather than bolted on afterwards. Digital ordering kiosks, smart LED lighting, contactless payment zones, and acoustic management systems can all be integrated as seamless design enhancements that improve both the guest experience and day-to-day operations.

Practical Implementation Tips

Bringing these trends to life doesn't have to mean a complicated rollout. A few practical principles go a long way:

  • Plan well in advance so design decisions align with your build timeline
  • Use modular components that can be reconfigured as your needs evolve
  • Partner with experienced designers and fit-out specialists
  • Prioritise the areas that matter most to the guest experience
  • Keep the overall design simple to enable an efficient launch

The restaurants that stand out aren't necessarily the ones that chase every trend — they're the ones that thoughtfully combine the trends that fit their brand, their guests, and their operations into a space that feels considered and current.
