Harvest Salad & Co
Bengaluru

A complete QSR fit-out for Harvest Salad & Co, a health-focused salad café in Bengaluru — green-toned storefront with backlit channel-letter signage, a fully tiled service and display counter, channel-tufted booth seating, rattan pendant ceiling fixtures, arched wall niches with wood panelling, and a custom branded retail display unit — taken from bare shell to opening-ready in 15 days.

LocationIndiranagar, Bengaluru
CategoryQSR / Café
ClientHarvest Salad & Co
Timeline15 Days
Project Overview

From Bare Shell to a Fresh, Branded QSR in 15 Days

Harvest Salad & Co needed a health-focused café fit-out that felt as fresh as the food on the menu — and needed it fast. FlatPackCo took the unit from a stripped concrete shell to a fully branded, opening-ready café in just 15 days, fabricating the entire Flatpack scope off-site while civil work progressed on the ground.

The entry sequence was rebuilt with a kitchen partition with door (H3000 x W1625mm), a glazed door section (H2400 x W4055mm), and a façade bulkhead (H750 x W4055mm) — all finished with wooden elements and a fluted wall panelling treatment on the entrance column, plus an MS-framed planter to greet guests at the door.

The service line is built around a continuous counter front partition with flap doors (H1050 x W9650mm) and matching left & right partitions, housing a POS counter, work top, back POS counter, packing counter, and a counter with sink. Above the back counter, a curved wall panel with integrated lighting (H1800 x W1900mm) frames the display, alongside a DMB stand and wooden wall elements.

The dining and retail zone carries the brand through a merch display unit (H2100 x W1350mm), a 13m run of printed wall panelling, curve and wall-side planters, a fluted panelling treatment on the column, and a community table with planter (H750 x W2175mm). Back-of-house is completed with framed doors for the kitchen, staff toilet, and a sliding staff room door. Loose furniture rounds out the space with two long booth sofas (5350mm and 3350mm), a ledge table, café tables, chairs, and high chairs.

How We Did It

Shell to Storefront in 15 Days

1
Entry, Glazing & Façade

The kitchen partition with door, glazing with door, and façade bulkhead were fabricated and installed first, along with the fluted wall panelling on the entrance column and the MS-framed planter at the entrance.

2
Counter & Service Line

The front, left & right counter partitions with flap doors went up around the POS counter, work top, back POS counter, packing counter, and counter with sink — finished with the lit curved wall panel and DMB stand above the back counter.

3
Walls, Planters & Loose Furniture

Printed wall panelling, the merch display unit, curve and wall-side planters, the community table, kitchen/staff doors, and the loose furniture set — booth sofas, tables, chairs, and ledge table — were installed and styled ahead of opening.
