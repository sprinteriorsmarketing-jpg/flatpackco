RECOMMENDED IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS
---------------------------------
  Hero backgrounds:    1920×1080px minimum, JPG, 80% quality
  Page heroes:         1920×800px minimum, JPG, 80% quality
  Project images:      1200×900px minimum, JPG, 85% quality
  Product images:      800×600px minimum, JPG, 85% quality
  Blog featured:       1200×630px (Open Graph compatible), JPG, 85% quality
  Client logos:        SVG preferred, or PNG with transparent background, min 300px wide
  Thumbnails:          400×400px, JPG or PNG
