Core Areas

Three Elements That Complete Every Fit-Out

Floors, dado walls, and restrooms — the three areas that guests experience with every visit, yet too often receive only generic treatment.

Raised Platform Flooring

Raised platforms transform a flat floor plan into a dynamic, multi-level dining environment. A 150–200mm platform elevation defines VIP zones, bar areas, and feature dining sections — creating a sense of architectural drama and guest hierarchy that single-level layouts simply cannot achieve. We build platforms with integrated step lighting, safe edge nosings, and surface finishes that match your overall interior direction.

Bottom Wall Treatments

The lower 900mm–1200mm of any restaurant wall takes more punishment than any other surface — chair backs, cleaning chemicals, accidental scuffs. Our dado and bottom wall treatments address this with surface materials that are built for impact resistance: stone-effect laminates, ceramic tile systems, robust painted MDF panels, and premium wall cladding that looks as good in year five as it did on opening day.

Toilet & Washroom Fit-Outs

Your restaurant's toilets are the last touchpoint of every guest's experience — and guests remember them. FlatPackCo delivers complete washroom fit-outs: toilet cubicle partition systems, urinal screens, feature wall treatments, mirror frames, and accessory mounting — all designed to carry your brand's aesthetic through to its most intimate space.

Detailed Solutions

Every Element, Specified to Perfection

Raised Platform Construction

Engineered timber sub-frames with plywood decking and your choice of surface finish. Integrated wiring conduits for in-platform lighting, and precisely engineered edge transitions that are both safe and visually sharp.

LED Step Lighting

Recessed linear LED strip lighting integrated into platform edges and step nosings — adding atmospheric lighting at floor level while improving safety on level changes. Available in warm white, cool white, and RGB configurations.

Impact-Resistant Dado Panels

High-pressure laminate (HPL) and compact panel systems for lower wall cladding — rated for commercial impact resistance and chemical cleaning agents. Available in any finish from woodgrain to stone to solid colour.

Waterproof Wet Area Panels

Moisture-resistant wall panelling for kitchen pass-throughs, back-of-house wet walls, and toilet enclosures. Hygienic, grout-free panel systems that clean in seconds and will not warp, peel, or discolour over time.

Toilet Cubicle Partitions

Full-height and standard-height toilet cubicle partition systems in a range of materials — powder-coated steel, compact HPL, and timber-effect laminates — with concealed-hinge doors, coat hooks, and all necessary hardware.
