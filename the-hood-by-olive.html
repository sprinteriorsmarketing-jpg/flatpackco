The Hood by Olive
Wood Street, Bangalore

A three-zone fit-out for Olive Bar & Kitchen on Wood Street, Bangalore — a 9.3-metre rustic bar counter, acoustic ceiling, European-style millwork, bespoke loose furniture, outdoor fire hood display, and a fully fitted basement entertainment zone.

LocationBangalore, Karnataka
CategoryBar & Kitchen

Project Overview

Three Floors. One Seamless Brand Identity for Olive Bar & Kitchen

The Hood by Olive on Wood Street, Ashoknagar, is one of Bangalore's most iconic dining destinations, and this fit-out had to match that pedigree. FlatPackCo was engaged by Olive Bar & Kitchen Private Limited as the millwork and furniture partner for a comprehensive three-zone transformation covering the main indoor dining and bar area, an outdoor display zone, and a basement entertainment level.

The centrepiece of the indoor space is a 9.325-metre bar counter built with rustic metal skirting, vertical strip solid wood, and horizontal veneer bands — a statement piece that anchors the entire ground floor. Flanking it is a 3.365-metre-tall vinyl record VNR laminate storage unit, acoustic panel ceiling suspension, and three full walls of two-layer wainscot panelling with service hooks. European-style mock windows in artwork and distress-painted louvered shutters layer in the architectural character that Olive is known for.

The loose furniture package, delivered under a separate invoice, adds 8 two-seater teak sofas with valvate fabric upholstery, booth sofas in leather tufted design, a curved long booth sofa, wingback lounge chairs, benches, and seven table types ranging from mosaic-tile tops to teak industrial polish, all coordinated to a single design language.

What We Delivered

Three Zones, Eight Work Streams

Bar Counter & Fixed Millwork

9.325m rustic bar counter with metal skirting, solid wood strips & veneer bands; 3.365m vinyl record storage unit; BOH window frame with fluted glass; service table.

Wainscot & Acoustic Ceiling

Acoustic panel ceiling (H450 × W4800 × D2400mm); 2-layer laminate wainscot on three walls (4m left, 8m right, 9.45m kitchen) with top band and service hooks.

Architectural Millwork

European-style mock windows in louvered distress paint (music area); 3 kitchen wall mock windows; piano dining seating with glass top (3.6m); 4 circular booth panelling in Duco paint.

Outdoor Zone

Fire hood display with service station, mirror & wooden logs (H1800 × W1155mm); 2 display racks with glass shutters & lit; 8 artificial VNR rafters; mirror etching element with frame.

Basement Zone

Washroom doors HIS & HER; 3 vision panel doors; wall panelling with vinyl & storage boxes (H2700 × W1600mm); antique mirror; library display (H2700 × W2000mm); wainscot panelling in 3 areas.

Loose Seating

8× two-seater teak sofas (valvate fabric); 2× booth sofas leather tufted; 1× curve long booth; 2× wingback lounge chairs; teak benches (rough polish & distress paint); fabric puffies.

Loose Tables

Wall-mounted (TB-01); teak industrial rough polish (TB-02); round composite stone (TB-03); round VNR laminate (TB-04); VNR laminate rect. (TB-05); mosaic tile (TB-06); VNR laminate rect. (TB-07).

Skirting, Chairs & Finishing

360 rft metal skirting in wooden grain powder coat; teak rope-cotton chairs (CH-02 ×4); leatherite stools (CH-03 ×2); door architraves; window jambs VNR finish (×4).

How We Did It

Three Workstreams Running in Parallel

1
Design Lock & Off-Site Fabrication

All millwork drawings approved, laminates and finishes signed off before procurement. The bar counter, wainscot panels, storage units, doors, and furniture frames were fabricated simultaneously in a controlled workshop environment.

2
Fixed Millwork Installation

Bar counter, acoustic ceiling, wainscot panelling, outdoor fire hood display, and basement library & vision panel doors installed zone by zone as civil handover progressed.

3
Loose Furniture, Mock Windows & Finishing

Loose furniture positioned and dressed; European mock windows fitted; metal skirting run; all snagging cleared and keys handed over to Olive's operations team.
