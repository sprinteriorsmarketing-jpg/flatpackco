Ocean 11 Restaurant
Andheri, Mumbai

A full ocean-themed interior fit-out for Ocean 11 Restaurant in Marol — 659 sqft bamboo ceiling with solidwood framing, 5 infinity mirrors, stingray wall, surfboard ocean wall installation, pinewood fluted staircase, custom bar counter, and a bespoke 2-seater boat-shape centrepiece.

LocationMarol, Andheri East
CategoryRestaurant
Year2024
Project Overview

Mumbai Gets an Ocean-Inspired Dining Destination

Ocean 11 in Marol, Andheri East isn't just a restaurant — it's an immersive marine environment. FlatPackCo was brought in to execute the complete interior fit-out, translating a bold ocean theme into every element: from ceiling to floor, bar counter to feature walls.

The centrepiece of the space is a 659 sqft bamboo ceiling with solidwood framing — a sweeping canopy that immediately sets the underwater ambience. Five infinity mirrors are positioned to amplify depth and create the sensation of boundless ocean space. The stingray wall and ocean wall with surfing board are custom fabricated installations that bring the marine world into the dining room.

The bar counter features a dedicated service platform, a fixture above the counter, and a full back display — all integrated into the ocean-themed language. The staircase is clad in pinewood fluting with fish vinyl artwork. Glazing with glass doors (144.5 sqft) opens up the space, while a mesh grill and planter with storage add texture and greenery. The boat-shape seating unit — a bespoke 2-seater — serves as a statement piece that completes the immersive experience.

How We Did It

Theme-First, Structure Second

1
Shop Drawing & Theme Alignment

Design drawings approved within one week of PO. Every element was reviewed against the ocean narrative — bamboo ceiling spec, stingray wall proportions, boat seating dimensions — before a single piece of material was procured.

2
Parallel Production — Ceiling, Walls & Bar

Bamboo ceiling framework, feature wall fabrication (stingray, surfboard), bar counter millwork, and infinity mirror sourcing were all run simultaneously during the 4-week production window.

3
Site Dress & Handover

Ceiling installed first to set the tone; feature walls, mirrors, and glazing followed. Boat seating positioned last as the hero piece. Site readiness prerequisites (paint, flooring, lights) coordinated with client before dispatch.
