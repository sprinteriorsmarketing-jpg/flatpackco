Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

Is prefabricated the same quality as traditional build?

Yes — in many cases better. Factory-controlled conditions eliminate weather delays, material inconsistency, and on-site skill variation. You get the same finish, every time.

Can you handle custom designs and brand guidelines?

Absolutely. Every project starts with your brief. We handle bespoke joinery, branded signage, custom colour palettes, and franchise-standard rollouts equally well.

Do you work outside Mumbai?

Yes. We have completed projects across Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Goa, Chennai, and Delhi. We service PAN India for projects of the right scale.

What if the project takes longer than 15 days?

15 days is our minimum for smaller spaces. Larger or more complex projects have longer timelines, agreed upfront — always in writing, always fixed.
