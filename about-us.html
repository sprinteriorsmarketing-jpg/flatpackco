The Origin

Built From an Industry Insight, Not a Business Plan

FlatPackCo did not start as a company. It started as a frustration. After decades inside the hospitality industry, we watched the same problems repeat on every project — fragmented teams, misaligned timelines, budgets that drifted, and quality that varied from site to site.

The issue was never a lack of talent. It was the structure of the process itself. Restaurant development had always been assembled from separate, disconnected parts — and nobody had built the system to integrate them.

FlatPackCo exists because we did. The FlatPack Method brings design, engineering, procurement, manufacturing and execution into one coordinated framework — creating the kind of predictability, quality and speed that restaurant brands need to grow with confidence.

Meet the Founder

From Industry Insider
to Category Creator

After more than two decades inside the hospitality industry — designing restaurants, running manufacturing operations, and leading project delivery across India — Sona Mantri arrived at a conclusion that was both obvious and urgent: the way restaurants get built is fundamentally broken. And the problem is not the people. It is the structure.

Her career had given her a perspective that few founders can claim. She had seen the problem from every angle — the designer waiting on the engineer, the contractor waiting on the vendor, the restaurant owner watching their opening date slip while five different parties pointed at each other. The fragmentation was not an accident. It was how the industry had always operated. Nobody had questioned it.

"Every restaurant brand I've worked with needs the same three outcomes: open on time, stay on budget, and maintain quality across every location. The industry was never structured to deliver all three. So I built a system that was."

— Sona Mantri, Founder & CEO

The FlatPack Method did not emerge from a whiteboard exercise. It was shaped by thousands of real projects — by understanding exactly where the handoffs fail, where the timelines drift, and where the cost overruns hide. By integrating design, engineering, procurement, manufacturing and execution into a single accountable system, Sona removed the coordination gaps that define the traditional model.

Her approach blends technical precision with deep hospitality instinct. She understands that a restaurant is not a construction project. It is a business asset. Every design decision, every material choice, every timeline commitment ultimately affects revenue, brand perception and the guest experience. FlatPackCo was built with that understanding at its core.

What drives the company today is the same conviction Sona started with: that restaurant brands deserve a delivery partner as serious about their growth as they are. Not a vendor. Not a contractor. A system that treats every project as if the founder's reputation depends on it — because it does.
