Turnkey Delivery

What a Turnkey Restaurant Contractor Does

A turnkey restaurant contractor takes responsibility for delivering a finished space to an agreed scope, so you manage one partner instead of separate designers, carpenters and vendors. FlatPackCo works this way across India, with around 50 hospitality projects delivered.

The difference from a build-only contractor is who owns the gaps. When design, drawings, manufacturing and site work sit with separate teams, delays and mismatches often land back on the owner. A turnkey model puts that coordination with one team.

If you are still comparing delivery models, read Restaurant Contractor vs Design-Build. To see the stages in detail, visit our Restaurant Design & Build page.

Before You Sign

Questions to Ask Any Turnkey Contractor

Whichever contractor you choose, these questions will show how much responsibility they are really taking on:

  • Exactly what is included and excluded, including civil work, services, kitchen equipment and approvals?
  • Who is responsible if the drawings and the site conditions do not match?
  • What will be made in a factory, and what will be built on site?
  • How is the opening date set, and what could change it?
  • How are changes to the scope priced and approved?
  • Can I see completed projects of a similar size and format?

Frequently Asked Questions

What does turnkey mean for a restaurant project?

It means one contractor takes responsibility for delivering the finished space to an agreed scope. You work with one team rather than coordinating separate designers, fabricators and installers.

Does FlatPackCo include civil work and services?

Our project team coordinates installation, civil work and finishes on site. The exact civil, electrical, plumbing and HVAC scope is agreed for each project and written into your scope.

Who handles licences and landlord approvals?

Requirements vary by city, building and restaurant format. We agree during scoping who is responsible for each approval, so it is clear before work begins.

Where can you deliver turnkey restaurant projects?

We take projects across India. Our work includes Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Chennai and Goa.

How long does a turnkey restaurant project take?

It depends on scope and site condition. Suitable projects can be delivered in as little as 15 days, while larger or more complex projects take longer.

Can you work from a design I already have?

Share your drawings and we can discuss what is possible and which stages would suit your project.
