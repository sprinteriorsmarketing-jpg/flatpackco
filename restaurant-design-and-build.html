Overview

End-to-End Restaurant Design & Build Solutions by FlatPackCo

Building a successful restaurant requires more than a great menu and an attractive interior. It demands careful planning, coordinated execution, efficient project management, and a deep understanding of how hospitality businesses operate. Every decision, from the layout of the dining area to the placement of the kitchen equipment, plays a role in creating a seamless customer experience and ensuring smooth day-to-day operations.

At FlatPackCo, we provide end-to-end Restaurant Design & Build services that simplify the entire project journey. Instead of coordinating multiple consultants, contractors, manufacturers, and suppliers, restaurant owners work with one experienced team that manages the project from concept to completion.

Whether you're opening your first café, launching a fine-dining restaurant, expanding a QSR chain, or renovating an existing hospitality space, our integrated Design & Build approach delivers consistent quality, faster execution, and greater control throughout the project.

Definition

What is Restaurant Design & Build?

Restaurant Design & Build is an integrated project delivery method where one company is responsible for both the design and execution of a restaurant. Rather than hiring separate architects, interior designers, contractors, furniture vendors, and project managers, everything is managed under one coordinated team.

This approach streamlines communication, reduces delays, minimises costly design changes, and ensures the final space reflects the original vision. At FlatPackCo, our Design & Build process covers every stage of the project, from planning and technical coordination to custom manufacturing, installation, and final handover.

The Process

Our Restaurant Design & Build Process

Our structured restaurant design-and-build process brings all stages together, ensuring precision, consistent quality, efficient coordination, and smooth project delivery.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin by understanding your restaurant concept, menu, target audience, operational requirements, location, and business objectives.

02

Concept Development

Our team develops the overall design direction, including mood boards, material palettes, space planning concepts, and customer experience strategy.

03

Space Planning

Efficient layouts are created to optimise seating capacity, kitchen workflow, customer circulation, storage, and service areas.

04

Design Development

Detailed interior designs, furniture layouts, lighting concepts, finishes, and branding elements are developed.

05

Technical Documentation

Comprehensive architectural drawings, furniture details, MEP coordination, and construction documentation are prepared to support smooth execution.

06

Custom Manufacturing

Furniture, counters, joinery, wall panelling, signage, and other custom elements are manufactured with precision to ensure consistency and quality.

07

Site Execution

Our project management team coordinates installation, civil work, finishes, and quality control to deliver the project according to the approved design.

08

Final Handover

Before handover, every element is thoroughly inspected to ensure the completed restaurant is ready for operations.

Restaurant Design & Build Cost

The cost of a Restaurant Design & Build project depends on several factors, including:

  • Restaurant size
  • Project location
  • Design complexity
  • Material selection
  • Furniture requirements
  • Commercial kitchen scope
  • Custom manufacturing
  • Branding elements
  • Technical coordination

Rather than comparing projects solely on a cost-per-square-foot basis, it's important to consider long-term durability, operational efficiency, and overall value.
