Panel Systems

The Right Panel for Every Space & Purpose

Each panel system serves a distinct function — here's how to choose the right one for your project.

Wainscoting

A timeless lower-wall panel treatment typically applied from floor to 900mm–1200mm height. It adds formality, protects walls from chair scuffing, and introduces texture and colour to dining rooms, corridors, and lobbies.

Full-Height Cladding

Floor-to-ceiling wall cladding transforms an ordinary wall into a signature design element. Available in timber slats, stone-effect laminates, textured MDF, and upholstered fabric finishes — all fully custom to your space.

Space Dividers

Partition screens and space dividers that define zones without closing off a space. Ideal for creating private dining areas, separating bar and dining zones, or adding visual structure to large open-plan restaurants.

Acoustic Panels

Sound-absorbing wall panels that make a real difference in busy restaurant environments. Available in fabric-wrapped, perforated timber, and foam-core constructions — reducing echo and increasing guest comfort.

Backlit Feature Panels

Illuminated wall panels using translucent resins, frosted acrylic, or tensioned fabric with LED backlighting create a dramatic, upscale atmosphere — ideal for bar back walls, reception areas, and feature dining zones.

3D Textured Panels

Raised-relief and three-dimensional panel systems add depth and tactility to walls — geometric patterns, organic textures, and custom designs that make your space genuinely unique and brand-distinctive.
