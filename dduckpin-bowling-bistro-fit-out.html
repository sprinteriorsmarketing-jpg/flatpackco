DDUCKPIN
India's First Duckpin Bowling Bistro

A 6,000+ sq ft social-entertainment venue at Infiniti Mall, Malad West — FlatPackCo executed the complete interior fit-out across fixed furniture, counters, fire-rated doors and bespoke bowling-zone detailing, through four rounds of additional scope right up to the venue's opening.

Project Overview

Bringing Duckpin Bowling to India, Fit Out to Match

DDUCKPIN, at Infiniti Mall, Malad West, is India's first duckpin bowling bistro — a 6,000+ sq ft venue that brings the North American duckpin format to Indian consumers, alongside India's first 3D interactive snooker table, private karaoke rooms and a full-service café-bar. Founded by Rohan Mathur with backing from Complete Sports Management, the venue is built as a social hub where guests move between games, food and conversation rather than a conventional food court outlet.

FlatPackCo's base scope covered the fixed furniture and civil-interior package across the FOH, MOH and BOH zones: a low-height partition at the MOH counter with LED-lit facia, a partly glazed 'L' partition separating MOH from BOH, expanded-metal-mesh screening in black powder coat, art wall boxing at the back counter bar, a glass-enclosed FOH pod finished in fluted toughened glass (4160 × 2715 × 3250 mm), an oversized FRP bowling pin sculpture, mirror panelling, open-shelf display storage, and six doors including two 2-hour fire-rated kitchen and service doors.

As the design evolved through pre-opening walkthroughs, FlatPackCo delivered four further rounds of scope on a live site — POS counters and a toughened-glass entry door, janitor storage and toilet detailing, mirror panelling and stainless-steel corner guards around the bowling lanes, and height modifications to the community table and chairs — closing out in time for DDUCKPIN's opening.

How We Did It

Fixed Fit-Out Executed in Parallel With a Live Opening Deadline

1
MS Framework & Partition Fabrication

Anti-rust treated MS framework fabricated for the low-height MOH partition, the glazed 'L' partition between MOH and BOH, and the expanded-metal-mesh screen — clad in HDHMR / fire-retardant plywood ready for laminate, micro-concrete and glass finishes.

2
Counters, Pods & Bespoke Elements

Back and front counters finished in leather-finish granite, the fluted-glass FOH pod, open-shelf display storage, the service station and the oversized FRP bowling pin built and installed in parallel.

3
Fire-Rated Doors & Phased Additional Works

2-hour fire-rated kitchen and service doors installed to code, followed by four rounds of additional scope — POS counters, bowling-area panelling, stainless corner guards and furniture modifications — delivered on a live site through to opening.
