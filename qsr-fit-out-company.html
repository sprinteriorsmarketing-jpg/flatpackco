Factory-built counters, seating, signage and interiors for quick service restaurants, delivered fast and repeatable from one outlet to the next.

QSR Fit-Outs

Built for Speed, Service and Repeatability

FlatPackCo designs, manufactures and installs QSR interiors across India. Our Harvest Salad & Co outlet in Bengaluru went from bare shell to opening-ready in 15 days, and we have delivered multiple outlets for The Croffle Guys.

QSRs usually work with small units, tight budgets and little tolerance for a late opening. Factory manufacturing suits this well: the counter, seating, wall panels and signage can be made while the site is prepared, then assembled quickly.

Once a format is drawn and approved, the same details can be used again for later outlets. Read our Restaurant Rollout Guide to see how that works across a growing brand.

Case Study

From Bare Shell to Opening-Ready in 15 Days

Harvest Salad & Co needed a health-focused café in Indiranagar that felt as fresh as its menu, and needed it quickly. We fabricated the Flatpack scope off site while civil work progressed on the ground.

  • Green-toned storefront with backlit channel-letter signage
  • Fully tiled service and display counter
  • Channel-tufted booth seating and rattan pendant fixtures
  • Arched niches with wood panelling and a branded retail display unit

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a QSR fit-out take?

It depends on scope, site readiness and approvals. Suitable projects can be delivered in as little as 15 days. Harvest Salad & Co in Bengaluru went from bare shell to opening-ready in 15 days.

Can you repeat the same design across outlets?

Yes. Once a format has been drawn and approved, its details can be manufactured again for later outlets, and adapted to each site where needed.

Have you delivered multiple outlets for a QSR brand?

Yes. We have delivered multiple outlets for The Croffle Guys.

Do you work outside Mumbai?

Yes. We take QSR projects across India. Components are made at our factory in Wada, transported to site and assembled there.

Can you supply just a counter or furniture for my QSR?

Yes. We can supply individual elements such as counters and furniture, as well as complete fit-outs.
