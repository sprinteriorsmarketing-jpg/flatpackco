Project Overview

Mumbai's Biggest Sports Bar Gets a World-Class Furniture Package

Amoeba at Phoenix Market City, Kurla, is one of Mumbai's most expansive entertainment destinations — combining a sports bar, bowling alley, and multi-zone seating across a massive floor plate. H. M. Leisure partnered with FlatPackCo to supply and deliver the complete furniture package, covering three distinct zones: the bowling area, the main dining floor, and the exclusive VIP lounge.

The bowling zone required a careful balance of comfort and durability — large L-shape sofas in leatherite with wooden legs for 8 banks, centre tables with Evo Cocoa Chips and Corian D-shape tops, and an amphitheater seating configuration with individual seat and backrest pieces in Euro leather. The two VIP L-sofas in Solanki Bentley upholstery anchor the premium zone alongside Vivanta Marble Gold centre tables.

The main dining floor is equally detailed: 90+ chairs across 5 styles — from Morocco leatherite dining chairs (46 nos) to MS black bar-height chairs — complemented by a 10-metre long booth sofa in Euro leather, a 6.2-metre sofa in Bentley fabric, back-to-back booth units in Nordic walnut laminate, and 9 table configurations spanning 600mm diameter circles to 1800×750mm six-seater rectangles.

How We Did It

Parallel Production Across Three Furniture Categories

1
Upholstery & Fabric Selection

All approved fabric samples (Morocco, Euro leather, Bentley) and Evo/Corian surface selections approved in writing before procurement. Foam densities and leatherite grades confirmed per zone requirement.

2
Parallel Frame & Table Production

Sal wood and plywood sofa frames, MS table legs, and table tops fabricated simultaneously. Composite marble, metallic panel, and Corian tops sourced and cut to specification concurrently.

3
Delivery & In-Site Placement

All furniture delivered and positioned zone by zone — bowling area first, then main dining, then VIP — minimising disruption to ongoing mall operations.
