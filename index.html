Ask Us Anything

How is FlatPackCo different from a traditional contractor?

A contractor is one piece of a fragmented process. You still need to coordinate designers, engineers, vendors and project managers separately. FlatPackCo integrates all five disciplines — design, engineering, procurement, manufacturing and execution — into one system with one team and one point of accountability. The result is faster timelines, consistent quality, and no coordination gaps.

We're planning to open multiple locations. Can this system scale?

Scaling is exactly what the system was designed for. Once your first location is delivered through The FlatPack Method, the design specifications, engineering standards and supply chains are already established. Each subsequent location becomes faster, more predictable and more cost-efficient. The system improves with repetition.

Do you only work with chains, or can individual restaurant founders use this?

The FlatPack Method works for a single location just as effectively as for a fifty-location rollout. Whether you're a first-time founder opening a café or an established chain expanding nationally, the same integrated approach applies — because predictable timelines, cost certainty and quality consistency matter at every scale.

What types of F&B formats do you work with?

We work exclusively within hospitality — restaurants, cafés, QSR formats, bars, cloud kitchens, food courts, and social dining concepts. This vertical focus is deliberate. The depth of F&B expertise we've built over 25 years and hundreds of projects is what makes The FlatPack Method possible.
