Kiosk Format Solutions

FlatPackCo kiosk formats are purpose-built for high-footfall retail environments where space is limited but brand impact must be maximum. From food court islands and mall corridor kiosks to airport grab-and-go units and transit hub pop-ups, every kiosk is designed for rapid assembly, full brand integration, and the operational demands of compact service environments. Pre-fabricated in our Mumbai workshop, shipped flat, and assembled on-site in as little as two days.

Kiosk Types We Build

  • Food Court Islands — central service kiosks with 360° branding and integrated equipment bays
  • Mall Corridor Kiosks — slim-profile units designed to maximise selling space within tight lease footprints
  • Airport & Transit Units — grab-and-go formats built for speed, hygiene, and regulatory compliance
  • Pop-Up & Event Kiosks — lightweight, quick-assembly structures for temporary retail activations
  • QSR Satellite Kiosks — compact brand extensions with full menu delivery capability
  • Beverage & Juice Bars — counter-integrated plumbing, refrigeration, and display zones

Applications

Kiosks Built for Every High-Traffic Environment

Food Courts

Central island and inline kiosks engineered for food court operations — equipment integration, ventilation-ready, and full brand wrapping on all visible faces.

Shopping Malls

Sleek corridor kiosks that maximise selling space within tight lease footprints — lockable storage, display shelving, and lit signage built in.

Airports & Stations

Fast-service units built for transit environments — grab-and-go layouts, easy-clean surfaces, and compliance with aviation and railway authority standards.

Events & Pop-Ups

Lightweight, tool-free assembly kiosks for brand activations, exhibitions, and seasonal pop-up retail — pack down flat for storage and reuse.

Design Approach

Engineered for Compact Performance

Every square inch counts in a kiosk. We design around your menu, equipment, and brand — not a standard template.

Space Optimisation

Every kiosk layout is planned around your equipment list and service flow — nothing wasted, every zone purposeful, and crew movement kept efficient even in the smallest footprints.

Full Brand Integration

Backlit signage panels, brand-colour fascias, menu board frames, and logo placements are designed into the structure — not stuck on as an afterthought.

Rapid Assembly

Pre-fabricated panels, knock-down joints, and numbered components mean your kiosk goes from flat-pack crates to fully operational in two to four days on-site.
