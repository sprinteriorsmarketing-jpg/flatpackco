Restaurant Furniture Manufacturer in India

Custom commercial furniture for restaurants, cafés, bars and hospitality venues, made at our factory in Wada and available with or without a fit-out.

Commercial Furniture

Furniture Built for Hospitality Use

FlatPackCo manufactures custom restaurant furniture at our factory in Wada. You can buy furniture on its own or as part of a complete restaurant fit-out, for projects across India.

Everything here is made for commercial use, where seating gets heavy daily wear and has to suit a specific brand and floor plan. We work with restaurant owners, hospitality groups, designers and contractors, rather than selling boxed home furniture.

Need help choosing? Our Restaurant Furniture Guide covers materials, layouts, comfort and durability. You can also browse our dine-in and movable furniture ranges.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy furniture without a complete fit-out?

Yes. We sell furniture on its own as well as part of a complete restaurant fit-out. Amoeba Sports Bar in Mumbai, for example, was a complete furniture package.

Is your furniture custom-made?

Yes. We make furniture to suit your layout, cover count, brand and preferred materials. Share your floor plan and references to get started.

Where is your furniture made?

Our furniture is made at our factory in Wada. Our office is in Andheri East, Mumbai.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We take projects across India, and our furniture and fit-out work includes Mumbai and Bengaluru.

Do you supply furniture for designers and contractors?

Yes. We can work with your designer or contractor and manufacture to their specification.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

Both depend on the pieces, quantity and finishes you need. Send your requirements and we will confirm them with your quote.
