Lighting Systems

Lighting is the single most powerful tool for setting mood, guiding attention, and making a space feel finished. FlatPackCo supplies and installs the full range of commercial lighting — from statement pendant clusters and custom chandeliers to architectural cove lighting, adjustable track systems, LED strip integration, and high-output task lighting for kitchens and service areas. We work with your interior designer or provide our own lighting layout, specifying fixtures by lux level, colour temperature, and dimming capability to match the exact atmosphere your space demands.

Design Approach

Layered Lighting, Precisely Specified

Great lighting is never one fixture — it's layers of ambient, task, accent, and decorative light working together.

Ambient Layer

The base wash of light that sets overall brightness and colour temperature — typically cove lighting, recessed downlights, or diffused ceiling fixtures matched to the mood of the space.

Accent & Feature Layer

Directional spots, track heads, and statement pendants that highlight focal points — art, texture walls, product displays, and bar areas where visual drama matters most.

Control & Dimming

Scene-setting dimmer systems that transition a space from bright daytime service to intimate evening ambience — one room, multiple moods, all at the touch of a panel.
